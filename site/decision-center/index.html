The Thesis

Trust before conversion. Every time.

Electrification has made HVAC confusing: expiring tax credits, five utility rebate programs, income-qualified tiers that haven't launched, panel questions, historic-home constraints. Homeowners aren't short on contractors — they're short on someone who will explain it straight.

The positioning statement: "We help homeowners make the right decision — even when that decision isn't immediate replacement."

Every tool on this page enforces that philosophy. Each result explains why it is what it is, names its assumptions, and flags when the honest answer is "wait," "repair," or "air-seal first." That's what earns return visits before the purchase, referrals after it — and citations from the AI systems homeowners increasingly ask first. Google and LLMs reward demonstrated expertise, original data, and structured local knowledge. Decision support is the visibility strategy.

What makes it impossible to copy

Any competitor can buy a redesign. None of them can fake proprietary local data compounding month after month.

Address-level rebate lookup

Enter an address → utility territory, live program amounts, income-qualified tiers, and current application status. The tool version of the page nobody in this market has even written.

Aerial thermal envelope intelligence

Drone thermal imaging that shows a homeowner exactly where their house leaks heat — informing right-sizing before any equipment quote. Original data no directory site can reproduce.

Real project library

Every install documented: neighborhood, housing era, equipment, permits, before/after performance. Case studies as proof — and as the training data for AI-powered guidance grounded in your own jobs.

Building-age intelligence

Decision trees by construction era: balloon framing, plaster walls, cast-iron radiators, no ductwork. The historic-home authority position, systematized.

BEPS & code radar

Building Performance Standards, permit requirements, and incentive changes tracked continuously — so homeowners, contractors, and even municipalities treat the platform as the reference.

Electrification roadmaps

Saved, phased plans — audit now, panel next year, heat pump when the furnace dies — so homeowners plan calmly instead of deciding in an emergency. Return visits are the metric.

Measure trust, not just leads: return visits, calculator sessions, saved plans, guide downloads.
