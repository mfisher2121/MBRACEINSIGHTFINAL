# First-touch drop (what actually gets sent)

HVAC owners in the DMV do not read a 19-file folder. They decide in under two minutes whether you already did the work.

Send **these five**. Keep everything else in the library until they ask.

## Send

1. **Leave-behind letter** — written about *their* Google profile, one unanswered review, one content gap. Template: `templates/leave-behind-letter.md`.
2. **Their GBP scorecard** — 0–100 vs the three companies ranking above them in their ZIP. Live format: `/example-report/`. Capture: `/report/`.
3. **The DMV Visibility Gap** — the homeowner questions the biggest operators haven't answered yet. `/visibility-gap/`
4. **Decision Center** — live rebate + heat-pump cost tools they can white-label. `/decision-center/`
5. **90-day GBP calendar** — posts they can run this week. `/playbooks/90-day-gbp-calendar.html?company=<Company>&date=<start date>`

## Do not send first

- All six systems playbooks at once
- Integration guide / system comparison (internal)
- HVAC outreach playbook (that's *your* process, not theirs)
- Content system maps

The playbooks stay available at `/playbooks/` after they engage.
